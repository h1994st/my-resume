> Sample job description for a fictional employer, used to demonstrate the tailoring workflow.

- **URL:** https://example.com/careers/senior-mouser
- **Captured:** 2026-09-30
- **Location / type:** On-site (grain warehouse), night shifts
- **Team / reports to:** Rodent Operations; reports to the Head Farmer

## About the team

Rodent Operations keeps 40 tons of grain safe from mice so the bakery downstream never runs out of
flour.

## Responsibilities

- Patrol the warehouse at night and detect rodent activity early.
- Respond to sightings quickly and report incidents to the farm staff.
- Keep the loading dock box inventory inspected and occupied.
- Train the barn's younger cats in patrol routes.

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years of hunting, pouncing, or equivalent laser-pointer experience.
- Excellent night vision and patience at the windowsill.
- Comfortable working independently on long, quiet shifts.

## Preferred qualifications

- Published research on rodent detection.
- Mentoring experience with kittens.
- Advanced degree in feline studies.

## Perks

- Unlimited cardboard boxes after every grain delivery.
- A sunny loft for naps between shifts.
